Account Deletion

Dwarkadheesh Apartments · Effective date: 22 November 2026

This page explains how residents can request deletion of their account for the Dwarkadheesh Apartments mobile app and the personal information associated with it.

Request deletion inside the app

  1. Sign in to the Dwarkadheesh Apartments app.
  2. Open My Profile.
  3. Tap Request Account Deletion and confirm the request.
  4. The request will be sent to the apartment administration for review and processing.

Request deletion if you cannot access the app

Send an account-deletion request to the society using the verified contact email below. Include your flat/house number and the email address or mobile number registered with the app. The administration may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account.

Society account-deletion email:
dwarkadheeshapartment@gmail.com

Do not send your password, UPI PIN, bank password, card details or other payment credentials.

What will be deleted

After verifying and processing a request, the administration will delete the user's Firebase Authentication account and associated personal account/profile information held for the app, including records that are no longer required for society operations.

Information that may be retained

Some payment, maintenance and accounting records may be retained where reasonably necessary for society accounting, dispute handling or applicable legal obligations. Any retained records will be limited to what is needed, access-restricted, and not exposed to a new resident of the same flat. Other personal information will be deleted when it is no longer required.

Processing and confirmation

The administration will review the request, verify account ownership where necessary, and process it as soon as reasonably practicable. If any information must be retained, the administration will explain the reason. Submitting a request does not instantly delete the account or automatically sign it out.
