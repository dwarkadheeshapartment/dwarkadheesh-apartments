Privacy Policy

Dwarkadheesh Apartments · Effective date: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Dwarkadheesh Apartments mobile application (the “App”) handles information when residents, administrators and security personnel use it. Please read it before using the App.

1. Who is responsible

Dwarkadheesh Apartments is responsible for the operation of the App and the apartment information managed through it. Privacy questions or requests can be submitted through the App’s Complaints & Support feature or in writing to the address listed at the end of this policy.

2. Information handled by the App

Depending on the features used, the App handles the following information:

  • Resident and account information: resident name, flat number, mobile number, email address, Firebase account identifier and account authentication information.
  • Security staff information: guard name, mobile number, account identifier and attendance records.
  • Maintenance and payment records: monthly/yearly maintenance status, amount, payment date, payment method, receipt details and payment history.
  • UPI verification details: UPI transaction reference/UTR and transaction date that a resident enters for the society administrator to verify.
  • Visitor and society operations: visitor name, visitor mobile number, visit purpose, entry status/time, emergency contact details, society notices and complaint messages/replies.
  • Administration records: administrator account identifier/email and activity records used to maintain accountability for administrative actions.

The App does not ask for or store UPI PINs, bank passwords or banking login credentials. Do not enter such credentials into the App.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate residents, administrators and security guards; manage flat and resident records; maintain monthly and yearly maintenance accounts; submit and review UPI payment references; record payments entered by administrators; generate receipts and reports; manage visitors, complaints, emergency contacts, notices and guard attendance; and protect the society’s records.

4. Service providers and external apps

The App uses Google Firebase Authentication for account authentication and Google Cloud Firestore for cloud database storage. Google processes information as a service provider under its applicable terms and privacy practices. Firebase may process technical information necessary to provide, secure and operate these services.

When a resident chooses to make a payment, the App opens an external UPI application. The selected UPI app/payment provider handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The App receives no UPI PIN or bank password. A resident may enter the UPI transaction reference and date into the App so the society administrator can check the payment against society records.

5. Sharing and advertising

Dwarkadheesh Apartments does not sell personal information. Information is available to authorised society administrators and relevant society personnel to perform the functions described in this policy. Information is processed by the service providers needed to operate the App. The reviewed App version does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in its declared app dependencies. This statement will be updated if the App’s integrations change.

6. Device permissions and files

The App requires internet access to communicate with Firebase. When a user requests a receipt or report, the App saves the generated PDF or report to the device’s Downloads area. On older Android versions, legacy storage permission may be requested to save these files. The reviewed App manifest does not request camera, microphone, contacts or device-location permissions. Files downloaded to the device can be managed or deleted by the device user.

7. Security

The App uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore access controls configured for the society project. Information sent to Firebase is transmitted using Firebase’s encrypted network connections. Access to society records is intended to be limited according to the user’s role and the configured database rules. Users should keep passwords confidential, sign out on shared devices and report suspected unauthorised access to the apartment administration. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Retention and deletion

Society records are retained for as long as reasonably needed to administer the apartment community, maintain payment and accounting history, handle complaints and security records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Residents may contact the apartment administration through Complaints & Support or in writing at the address below to ask about their information or request deletion. Some payment, accounting or security records may need to be retained where required for legitimate or legal reasons.

Important: Residents can request account deletion from the App using My Profile → Request Account Deletion. Residents who cannot access the App can use the contact method on the public Account Deletion page. A request is reviewed by the apartment administration; it is not an instant self-service deletion. The administration will process the Firebase Authentication account and associated personal information, subject to any records that must be retained for legitimate accounting, dispute-resolution or legal reasons. Any retained records will be restricted and not made available to a new resident of the same flat.

9. Children

The App is intended for residents, society administrators and security personnel. It is not designed for children to create independent accounts.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when App features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest effective date will be shown at the top of the policy. Continued use of the App after an updated policy is made available means the updated policy has been presented to users; any consent required by law will be obtained separately.
